## 1. Hook (EJ, 0:00-0:25)
- "Gesture tracking today means cameras. But cameras aren't allowed in patient rooms or bathrooms, and they fail in the dark."
- **[Move hand at the screen, the flower reacts]** "Right now this laptop is playing a sound you can't hear, and it can see my hand. No camera."

## 2. How it works (Yahya, 0:25-0:45)
- "The speaker's sound bounces off your hand into the mic. Toward the screen, the pitch rises; away, it drops; faster means a bigger shift. A small model turns that into gestures."
- "Every app shares one gesture: a quick double tap in the air means **undo**."

## 3. Care, Health (Ian, 0:45-1:25)

- **[Open and close hand]** "That's pain." **[Double tap]** "Wrong signal? Undo."
- "No lens, nothing recorded. Only the word *help* leaves the room."
