# Slides (5)

## 1. Echo: your laptop sees your hands without a camera
- Inaudible 20 kHz tone out of the speaker, echo into the mic, Doppler shift gives motion
- No camera, no new hardware, no images, works in the dark

## 2. Lock: a gesture password for a kid's browser
- Parent sets a secret hand rhythm; 3 wrong tries trigger an alarm and a parent alert
- Gets kids off the screen (Touch Grass, People First)

## 3. Care: a camera-free call button for patients
- Wave for help; caregiver dashboard lights up within 1 s
- Inactivity alerts and an activity timeline; privacy preserving (Health)

## 4. Habit and Undo
- Habit nudges on scratching or nail-biting
- Undo in every app: double tap toward the screen (Undo track)
- Built by 4 humans with 4 AI agents in 40 hours (Agent track)
