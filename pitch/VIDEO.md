# ECHØ: 2-minute demo video (shot list + script)

Follows the pitch order in SLIDES.md. Film the laptop screen (screen recording) plus a phone camera on the hands for the
gesture shots. Volume at max, quiet room, ECHØ open at http://localhost:5173. The webcam is covered with tape in every
hand shot.

## Before filming
- Set the Lock signs and lock the sites in advance (the setup takes too long on camera).
- Habit: press **Demo: on** so the hour chart and the 7-day row are full, and turn Habit on (10 s calibration) before rolling.
- Do two or three takes of each gesture shot and keep the cleanest; don't speed footage up or fake a detection.
- Record the voice-over separately in a quiet room and lay it over the cut.
