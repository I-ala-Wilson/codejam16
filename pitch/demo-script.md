# Pitch script (about 4 minutes, read aloud; the rest of the 7 minutes is Q&A)

Setup: the demo MacBook Pro on the table, volume at max, webcam taped over, ECHØ open on Home. Lock signs already set and
locked. Habit on with Demo data. The caregiver view open on a second screen or phone.

## 1. Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
"Cameras hit three walls. They aren't allowed in hospital rooms or bathrooms. They fail in the dark. And they record you.
ECHØ uses sound instead. Your laptop can hear your hands."

## 2. How it works (0:30 - 1:10)
(Scroll to the 3D explainer, then wave over the keyboard so the flower reacts.)
"The speaker plays a 20 kilohertz tone that's too high to hear. Your hand bounces it back slightly higher when it moves
toward the screen and lower when it moves away. That's the Doppler effect. A small model turns that echo into five
gestures. There's no camera and no image, and the audio never leaves this laptop."

## 3. Health: Care (1:10 - 1:50)
(Open Care, wave for about 2 seconds.)
"Health. For someone in a hospital bed or a bathroom who can't reach a call button, a wave is a help call. Open and
close your hand for pain." (Point at the caregiver view.) "The caregiver sees it instantly. No camera in the room, and it
works with the lights off."

## 4. Touch Grass and Undo Button: Lock (1:50 - 2:50)
(Open the blocked site.)
"Touch Grass and Undo Button. A parent blocks a site. To the kid it looks like the Wi-Fi is just broken, so they go
outside. Only the parent's secret hand signs open it." (Do the 4 signs; if one is wrong, double tap.) "If I make a
mistake, a double tap in the air undoes the last sign. That undo works everywhere in ECHØ. And there it opens."

## 5. Touch Grass: Habit (2:50 - 3:15)
(Open Habit, scratch your forearm for about 5 seconds.)
"Habit notices when you scratch while you work and gives you a soft nudge. The chart shows your day."

## 6. Agent Solution and honest numbers (3:15 - 4:00)
"Agent Solution. The model is 128 kilobytes and runs on this laptop's own CPU, in the browser. No server, no cloud
cost, and a new calibration trains in about three minutes. Four of us built it with five AI agents on one shared board.
Honest numbers: for a person the model has never seen, it gets about 45% of gestures. On this laptop, with a few minutes
of my own recordings, about 77%. ECHØ: your laptop can hear your hands."

## Q&A prompts to have ready
- "Does it record me?" No. Audio is analysed on the laptop and thrown away; only events like "help" leave it.
- "Will it work on my laptop?" Any laptop with a speaker and mic; weaker speakers need a short calibration.
- "Why not a camera?" Not allowed in many patient rooms and bathrooms, and it fails in the dark.
- "What did the AI do?" The agents wrote and tested most of the code; we chose what to build, recorded the data and tested it live.
