# Demo script (3:00, read aloud)

Setup: laptop on the table, volume up, webcam taped over, phone showing the caregiver dashboard.

## 1. Waterfall (0:00 - 0:30)
"Your laptop can see your hands without a camera. The speaker plays a tone you can't hear, and the mic listens to the echo off your hand. Judge, wave your hand over the keyboard. See the screen react? The webcam is taped over. No camera, no images, and it works in the dark."

## 2. Lock (0:30 - 1:30)
"Lock is a gesture password for a kid's browser. YouTube is blocked. Judge, try to unlock it with a gesture. Not it. Again. A third time: alarm, and the parent's dashboard gets an alert. Now I unlock it with our secret four-gesture rhythm." (perform it) "Unlocked. This is how we undo the mistake of handing a kid the iPad."

## 3. Care (1:30 - 2:30)
"Care is a camera-free help button. The patient waves for help." (wave) "On the caregiver's phone the alert lights up within a second. If nothing moves for too long, we raise an inactivity alert, and the timeline shows how restless or still the person was. No camera in the room."

## 4. Habit (2:30 - 3:00)
"Habit catches repetitive motion like scratching and fidgeting." (fake scratching) "The nudge chime fires and it's logged. Double tap toward the screen, like double-clicking the air, to undo it and snooze the nudge. Undo works the same way in every app."
